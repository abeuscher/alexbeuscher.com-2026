## Before the web

### Age 14 · Purity Supreme

Bagging groceries.

### Babysitting and tutoring

Through my mid and late teens I babysat a couple of times a week for several families. I tutored elementary school kids while I was in high school, then high school and college students while I was in college, mostly in writing and math. For many years I worked one on one, several times a week, with a student who had Asperger syndrome and severe OCD.

### Alden Merrell

A bakery, for my senior year of high school.

### Brigham's

Short-order cook, for six months.

### Kaplansky Insurance

Filing folders at an insurance agency in Brookline.

### The movie theater at Cleveland Circle

Usher, on and off for about eight years. I had a good announcing voice and could manage a crowd in a way people enjoyed.

### Mazemakers

A summer camp I had gone to as a kid, where I went back to work.

### Harvard

Tech support at the Graduate School of Education for about nine months, then work at other offices around the university, first through a temp agency and later through people I'd worked with. I did a good deal of consulting for the English department, teaching the staff to manage their own website.

## Boston

### 1994–1999 · Emerson College

A BFA in Writing, Literature & Publishing.

### 1997–2000 · AMICAS

AMICAS made software for viewing and managing medical images, such as X-rays, in a web browser on an ordinary PC. I was working there full time before I graduated, answering phones and running the website. As webmaster I redesigned the public website and built the intranet and the customer support center.

### 1998–present · Freelance

I've worked on and off as a freelance developer and designer ever since, alongside whatever else I was doing. Along with web work I've done basic IT consulting, application training, and print design. Clients have included film, healthcare, and environmental organizations.

### 2001–2003 · Solomon Schechter Day School

I taught video and web design to children aged 9 to 12 in the after-school program. In video, students learned to work a camera, plan a movie, and make short films. In web, we made a basic site using a text editor and graphics tools.

### 2001–2011 · MCO Productions

I ran pub trivia on and off for years, and worked weddings, parties, and special events as a DJ and MC.

### 2006–2007 · Nurse-Recruiter.com

An online job portal for nurses. I designed and built an intranet and redeveloped the site's software from Perl into PHP. I also rewrote most of the content for search value and for style, and in 10 months total visitors and goal conversions rose by more than 100%.

### 2007–2008 · Boston Interactive

A design firm in Charlestown. My work ran from markup and CSS layout to PHP projects and a good deal of JavaScript, for clients including FAO Schwarz, New England Coffee, and MedAssets.

### 2008–2011 · WAAF and Mike FM

Two Boston music radio stations owned by Entercom. I was Online Content Director. I managed a team of content producers and worked in a variety of roles to create video, web, and mobile content with our on-air personalities and with local and national celebrities. I built several novelty apps for the station sites, ran online contests and event microsites, and coached staff on blog writing and on engaging their audience over social media. Once the stations worked out that I could read copy, I also spent about an hour a week recording commercials, several a week for a couple of years, all of which aired.

## California

### 2011–2012 · Medley Health

A startup that provided membership-model practice management for primary care physicians. I ran the public websites, managed content, and did whatever web development was needed. The company is now defunct.

### 2013–2017 · 2K Games

2K publishes Borderlands, Civilization, and NBA 2K. I was the first full-time web developer in the marketing department; before I arrived, the sites were built through agencies. After I put basic processes and source control in place and tightened security, I was promoted and the team got a second developer. Together we unified the sites on a single headless CMS, set it up as a REST API, and built continuous deployment on AWS. That let us consolidate content editing and localization. The system was still in use years later and grew to support other parts of the 2K ecosystem.

### 2017–2020 · Dynamic Signal

A B2B software company, now called Firstup. I was in charge of the public web properties: the WordPress site, a support site on ZenDesk, a Marketo installation, and a content hub. The WordPress site had been online for four years and reworked by several agencies. I rebuilt the theme and took average page load from 14 seconds to 2.5. Along with development and maintenance, the work involved analytics, strategy, and content.

### 2019–2021 · Contentstack

A headless CMS company. I led a team of developers in Mumbai who built and supported the marketing and documentation sites. My time was split between managing and coaching the team, writing code, and working with stakeholders on a roadmap. We cut the codebase by about half. I also recorded voice-over and wrote copy for the docs.

### 2020–2023 · Skydio

A drone company. The marketing site ran on Eleventy and Sanity. I managed several contractors, worked with stakeholders to keep improving the site, and helped the security team with GDPR and CCPA compliance.

## Massachusetts again

### 2023–present · On my own

I work with nonprofits, small businesses, and individuals on websites, AI integration, and the technical problems that don't belong to anyone else. In 2026 I built NpHelper, an open-source CRM and website platform for small nonprofits.

## What ties it together

I enjoy bringing order to chaos. In most of these jobs I was the lead or the only technical person in the room, working directly with the people who needed something built, and the work was to turn what they needed into something that ran.
