A site needs care after launch. I handle updates, backups, security fixes, and small content changes, so you have one person to call when something needs doing.

The arrangement is simple:

- **You describe the problem** or the change you want.
- **I quote an estimate in hours** before I start. My rate is $100 an hour.
- **I do the work.** If anything changes the scope or the delivery date, we agree on a new estimate before I continue.

I'm happy to work under a written agreement.
