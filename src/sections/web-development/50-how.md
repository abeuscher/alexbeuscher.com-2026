I build with AI coding agents. I design the architecture, review the work, and own the finished code. That means senior-level judgment on every decision, at a pace and price a small team can afford.

I've also built with the models directly. In 2024 and 2025 I built the original prototype of MAIA for HIE of One, a patient-controlled medical AI assistant that lets someone ask questions of their own health record using the model they choose. It connected OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Mistral, and Together behind GNAP authorization, so the patient decided what each service could see. The [code for that first version](https://github.com/abeuscher/vue-ai-example) is public.
